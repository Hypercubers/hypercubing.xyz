# Higher-Dimensional Puzzles

In hypercubing, our primary focus is generalizing the notion of a twisty puzzle beyond 3D space. The best introduction to hypercubing is the standard [3×3×3×3](/puzzles/3x3x3x3.md) 4D Rubik's cube.

The recommended place to solve all 4D+ puzzles is [Hyperspeedcube](https://github.com/HactarCE/Hyperspeedcube) after the v2.0 update.

See the navigation sidebar for a list of puzzles that have wiki pages.
